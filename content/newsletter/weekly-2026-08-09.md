## Top Stories

### NU7 Coinholder Vote Opens August 25

The biggest governance event in Zcash since the original ZCAP polls. Sean Bowe (ebfull) announced on August 5 that Valar Group and Project Tachyon will coordinate a coinholder vote on the scope of NU7, running approximately 18 days from August 25 through September 12. Eligibility requires spendable shielded ZEC in the Ironwood pool at a blockchain snapshot on August 24 at 19:00 UTC — funds can move freely after the snapshot.

Five policy questions are on the ballot: whether to replace halvings with a smoother issuance curve (NSM), when transaction fee reissuance should begin, whether to deprecate the legacy Sprout format, whether to cut block times from 75s to 25s, and how aggressively to hold a September 30 implementation deadline. The results will only be recognized if at least 1,000,000 ZEC participates — roughly 5.9% of circulating supply. The Zcash Foundation separately announced that a ZCAP NU7 poll opens August 27. Two governance tracks, one upgrade. [Forum](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/nu7-token-holder-vote/56901) · [block2learn coverage](https://block2learn.com/2026/08/07/zcash-nu7-coinholder-vote-million-zec-upgrade/)

### Ironwood Crosses $1 Billion

Twelve days after activation, the Ironwood pool crossed $1 billion in value on August 8 — making it the largest shielded pool on Zcash, overtaking Orchard. Over half of all Orchard ZEC has now migrated through the turnstile. The pool holds 2.1M+ ZEC at time of writing, up from 828K a week ago. Migration velocity remains strong: 250,000+ ZEC crossed in a single 24-hour period on August 7 alone. Every ZEC that moves is one more coin with formally verified circuit guarantees.

### Zcash Foundation Q2 2026 Report

The Foundation published its second-quarter report on August 6, covering the full arc from the Orchard bug discovery in late May through Ironwood's clean activation on July 28. The engineering team shipped seven Zebra releases during the quarter, cut full sync time to ~14 hours, finalized FROST v3.0.0 with automatic key zeroization, and began early NU7 and Ironwood work. ZF also formally assumed stewardship of the Zcash GitHub organization, the z.cash domain, and the @Zcash handle on X — partnering with ZecHub to manage the account day-to-day. [ZF Report](https://zfnd.org/zcash-foundation-q2-2026-report/)

### ZCG August 3 Meeting: Eight Grants Declined

The committee cleared a long backlog, declining eight grants: Ironwood Sentinel (#376), Zcash UK & Ireland (#374), VibeQuest (#373), ZIP-229 Conformance Suite (#372), Zebra Mempool Adversarial Resilience (#362), Legitimacy Beyond Transparency (#360), FROST Threshold Custody (#358 — second attempt after #351 declined in July), and Zcash Belgium Chapter (#384). ZCG is tightening criteria: "lacking an end-to-end application" was cited repeatedly. No new approvals this cycle. [Forum #56909](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zcash-community-grants-meeting-minutes-8-3-2026/56909)

## Privacy Corner: Why Timing Breaks Privacy

You've shielded your ZEC. You're in the Ironwood pool. You feel private. Then you receive ZEC and deshield it 90 seconds later to pay a merchant.

You're no longer private.

Timing attacks are one of the most underappreciated privacy failures in shielded systems. The shielded pool hides amounts and addresses — but it can't hide the fact that you received funds and then spent them shortly afterward. If an observer can see both the inflow (shielding transaction) and the outflow (deshielding transaction) on the same address cluster, and those two events happen within minutes, the ambiguity set collapses. You go from "one of thousands of possible senders" to "probably this wallet."

The defense is time. Staying in the shielded pool longer — days, not seconds — means your ZEC mixes into a much larger anonymity cohort. Every block that passes with your ZEC still shielded is another block of cover traffic you're hidden inside. CipherScan's privacy risk tracker monitors exactly this pattern: shield-to-deshield sequences with tight timing windows are the most common high-risk pattern on the network right now, with multiple HIGH-rated transactions occurring daily.

Practical rule: if you're shielding ZEC specifically to spend it immediately, you haven't gained meaningful privacy. Shield early, hold in the pool, spend later. The gap between shield and spend is where your privacy lives.

*Shield your ZEC. Stay in the pool longer.*
