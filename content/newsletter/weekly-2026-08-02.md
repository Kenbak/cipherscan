## Top Stories

### Ironwood Week One: The Numbers

Five days since activation at block 3,428,143. Here's what the chain shows:

**Migration totals:**
- 847,865 ZEC has entered the Ironwood pool across 6,022 transactions
- 828,508 ZEC remains (net of 19,357 ZEC already spent from within Ironwood)
- Migration velocity: ~6,800 ZEC/hour (decelerating from day-one peak of ~31,000 ZEC/hour)
- 22% of Orchard's pre-activation balance has moved

**Where it came from:**
- 85.5% from Orchard (724,727 ZEC — 4,013 txs)
- 8.5% from Transparent (72,153 ZEC — 1,910 txs)
- 6.0% from Sapling (50,939 ZEC — 62 txs)

**Who migrated:**
- 146 transactions (1K+ ZEC each) account for 92.1% of all volume
- 2,621 transactions were under 1 ZEC — dust-testers and privacy-cautious users
- The median migration was a fraction of 1 ZEC; the mean was 212 ZEC

**Milestone — Ironwood > Sapling (July 31):** The new pool overtook Sapling's balance in under 72 hours. Sapling was activated in October 2018 — eight years of deposits surpassed in three days. Ironwood is now Zcash's second-largest shielded pool, behind only Orchard.

No failed transactions. No consensus issues. No chain splits. Supply verification has matched Zebra's authoritative state at every block since activation. [CipherScan Ironwood tracker](https://cipherscan.app/ironwood) · [ZODL article](https://zodl.com/ironwood)

### A Compliance Bridge for Shielded Zcash

Deborah Olaboye filed ZCG grant #379 ($17,500) proposing a Rust library and TypeScript SDK that lets exchanges ingest a Unified Full Viewing Key, scan shielded blocks, and produce FATF Travel Rule records — without ever gaining spend authority. The proposal cites 73 exchange delistings of privacy coins in 2025 and includes a working proof-of-concept (UFVK scanning, Sapling round-trip, Compliance Reference encode/decode). Marked 👀 Ready For ZCG Review. [Forum](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/grant-application-shielded-compliance-bridge/56862) · [GitHub #379](https://github.com/ZcashCommunityGrants/zcashcommunitygrants/issues/379)

### ZODL Explains the 60-Day Sprint

Josh Swihart (ZODL Executive Director) posted a thread on X explaining how the ecosystem went from discovering the Orchard bug to shipping a formally-verified replacement pool in under 60 days. ## Privacy Corner: Commerce Requires Opacity

A single payment reveals more than you think. If your supplier can see your revenue, they'll raise prices. If your competitor can see your payroll, they'll poach your staff. If your landlord can see your balance, they'll raise your rent.

This isn't hypothetical. On transparent blockchains, every transaction is public by default. Run a business on Bitcoin or Ethereum and anyone with a block explorer can reconstruct your entire financial life: who pays you, who you pay, how much you hold, and when you're vulnerable.

Zcash's shielded pools break this chain. Ironwood — three days old and already securing over $390 million — uses formally verified circuits and zero-knowledge proofs to let you transact without exposing any of this. Commerce has always required opacity to function. The difference now is that opacity has to be built, not assumed.

*Shield your ZEC. Stay in the pool.*
